# Youth-smoking-analysis
-This project analyzes CDC YRBSS data to identify which demographic and behavioural factors most strongly predict current cigarette use among US high-school students, and whether smoking prevalence has changed across recent survey cycles.
# research Questions-
-What is the prevalence of current smoking among youth, and how has it trended across survey years?
-Which demographic factors (age, sex, grade) are associated with smoking status?
-Are other risk behaviours (alcohol use, low physical activity, poor mental health) correlated with smoking?
-Can we predict smoking status from these factors, and which factors matter most?
